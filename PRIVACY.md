# Privacy

SnoreLog works on your phone only. It has no internet access and sends nothing anywhere.

**Microphone:** audio is recorded only while monitoring is armed and conditions are met. Clips, loudness curves, tags and statistics are stored in the app's private storage. You can delete a clip in the app, set how long clips are kept, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

**Backup:** a ZIP file is created only when you tap *Back up*, and saved where you choose. SnoreLog does not send it anywhere.

SnoreLog is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep apnea or any other condition.

The only network action in the app is the optional *Healthy sleep habits* button, which opens a web page in your browser when you tap it.
